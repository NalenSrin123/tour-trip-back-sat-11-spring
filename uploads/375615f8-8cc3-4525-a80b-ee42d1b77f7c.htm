Kampot Tours

Kampot is a small town in the south of Cambodia. It’s a sleepy riverside town but very popular with tourists and visitors. There are many bars and restaurants to enjoy in the evening, there are pepper farms, caves, national parks and mountains to see during the day.Also, you’re just a short ride away from the undeveloped seaside town of Kep.

Kampot FAQ!

Whether you’re looking to spending a few days exploring the town, riding to the top of Bokor Mountain or getting off the beaten track with a trip out into the countryside, it’s easy to see why visitors love Kampot.

Where can I buy Kampot Pepper?

Kampot produces some of the best red, black and white pepper in the world. You can see how it’s grown and processed. You can buy pepper directly from the farms you visit on a tour or you’re likely to find it in just about every gift shop you visit in town.

How can I get to Kampot?

Most guests will arrive in Kampot from either Sihanoukville or Phnom Penh. There are regular buses, shared taxis and private taxis. Angkor TK can arrange transportation to Kampot if required.

Where to stay in Kampot?

There are many hotels, resorts and guesthouses in Kampot. There’s something to suit all budgets. Most guests who book day tours with us have already booked their own accommodation. If you need help finding accommodation, Angkor TK will be happy to help.
